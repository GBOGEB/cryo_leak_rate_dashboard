🔍 Mitigation Details

RISK-001 — Iran Conflict → Helium Supply: Strategic helium reserve (3-month buffer), dual-source contracts, helium recovery system optimization

RISK-002 — LNG/LPG Market Coupling: Monitor LNG futures, establish He-specific supply agreements decoupled from LNG spot

RISK-003 — LOOP Event (Loss of Instrument Air): Backup air supply (accumulator tank), fail-safe valve positions verified, LOOP recovery procedure tested annually

RISK-004 — Cascade Valve Failure: Pressure relief valves, leak monitoring system, predictive maintenance schedule

RISK-005 — Extended MTTR (Spare Parts): On-site spare inventory (10% cold, 5% warm), multiple qualified suppliers

RISK-006 — Warm Valve Leak Rate Derogation: GBO calculation to verify acceptable loss rate, new sub-class with higher allowable rate, evaluate Cryoworld variants

RISK-007 — Seal Degradation under Radiation: Radiation testing program, preventive seal replacement at PM intervals

RISK-008 — Helium Price Volatility: Monte Carlo cost modeling, budget contingency at P90, hedging contracts

RISK-009 — ASME/PED Compliance Gap: Legal review of PED 2014/68/EU applicability, document derogation rationale

RISK-010 — Beam Downtime from Valve Failure: Redundant valve paths, hot standby valves, rapid isolation procedures

Geopolitical & Supply Risk Scenarios

🌍 Iran Conflict Scenario

Military escalation disrupts Qatar helium production (30% global supply). Algeria and US sources insufficient. He price spike 2-3× baseline.

Impact: €/kg rises from €120 to €300+. Annual cost increase: 150-200%.

⛽ LNG Market Coupling

Helium is byproduct of LNG processing. LNG market disruption cascades to He supply. Price correlation with natural gas futures.

Mitigation: He-specific supply contracts decoupled from LNG spot pricing.
