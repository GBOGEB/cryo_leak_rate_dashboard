Actuation Methods — Detailed

🔵 Pneumatic Actuation (Q1, Q2d, W1d)

  • Requires instrument air supply (6 bar(g))
  • Fail-safe position on air loss
  • Indirect actuation via pilot valve
  • Q1: Piezo pilot + electronic positioner (0-100%)
  • Q2d: Piezo pilot + limit switches
  • W1d: Solenoid pilot + limit switch 0%

🟡 Direct Electrical (W3)

  • No instrument air required
  • 24V solenoid direct actuation
  • Limit switch at 0% (closed)
  • Linear Kv characteristic
  • Radiation hardness: not required
  • Note: tbc if Asco or Bürkert can be 1LS
