WCS.HP Supply Protection — Overview

The WCS.HP (Worst Case Supply — High Pressure) protection system ensures that sidestream activities (recovery, purification, research taps) do not compromise the main 14 barg HP helium supply to QVB / WSH / beam cooling.

Scenario Details

Scenario 1: Normal Operation

All 3 compressors available (2 running, 1 standby). HP header stable at 14.0 barg. Sidestreams open. Total leak: 9×10⁻⁶ mbar·L/s (within budget). Full beam operation.

Scenario 2: WCS — 1 Compressor Failed

2 compressors running at 100% capacity, no standby margin. HP header drops to ~13.8 barg. Sidestreams automatically closed by interlock (P < 13.5 trigger may not yet activate, but precautionary closure reduces leak budget to 7×10⁻⁶). Full beam operation maintained.

Scenario 3: Emergency — 2 Compressors Failed

1 compressor running at 50% capacity. HP header at 13.2 barg (below LOW-LOW). Alarm raised. Beam reduced to 50% intensity to match available capacity. Total leak budget halved to 3.5×10⁻⁶.

Scenario 4: Total Failure

All compressors failed. Emergency beam shutdown. Vent to recovery system. Minimise He loss through controlled depressurisation.
