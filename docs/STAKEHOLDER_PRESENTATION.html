Technical Summary — Plain Language

What are helium leak rates?

Helium leaks are measured in incredibly small units (mbar·L/s). For perspective:

MeasurementMeaningLike…
1×10⁻⁸ mbar·L/s0.004 g helium lost per yearOne drop of water over 3 years
1×10⁻⁵ mbar·L/s3.85 g helium lost per yearOne teaspoon over 5 years
1×10⁻⁴ mbar·L/s38.5 g helium lost per yearOne tablespoon per year

RTM Requirement

System must lose < €45/year in helium (RTM-048 cap at 1×10⁻⁵ mbar·L/s)

Our Design Result

Calculated helium cost: €31/year (30% margin below limit) ✅

Key Insight

Valve leak rates have negligible impact on helium inventory. The warm valve derogation (cheaper HDPE valves instead of expensive bellow-sealed) saves €320,000 CAPEX with only €13/year extra helium cost.
